## Self-hosted is free

If you run Vibrail on your own machine or server, there's **nothing to pay** and
no billing area. You only pay for your own infrastructure (the server, bandwidth,
and any third-party services you choose to run). Vibrail itself is open source
under the Apache 2.0 license.
